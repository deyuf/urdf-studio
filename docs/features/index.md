# Guides

Each guide covers one thing you might want to do with a robot model. They
apply to both the browser and VS Code; differences are called out where
they exist.

## Looking at the robot

- [Navigate the 3D view](./viewport.html) — orbit, zoom, camera presets,
  grid and axes.
- [Visual and collision geometry](./render-modes.html) — compare the two
  layers, spot self-collisions.
- [Frames, labels and inertia](./overlays.html) — see joint frames, names
  and mass distribution in 3D.
- [Inspect a link](./inspector.html) — the link tree and per-link details.
- [View MuJoCo (MJCF) models](./mjcf.html) — open MuJoCo models read-only.

## Working with the model

- [Pose the robot](./joints.html) — joint sliders, limits, mimic joints,
  xacro parameters.
- [Find and fix problems](./diagnostics.html) — the Checks tab and how to
  act on it.
- [Edit the source](./editing.html) — change the URDF with live preview.
- [Measure, reach and collision pairs](./tools.html) — distances,
  workspace sampling, SRDF `disable_collisions`.

## Keeping and sharing results

- [Save poses and export](./poses.html) — saved poses, bookmarks, images,
  JSON, CSV and PDF.
- [Packages, xacro args and settings](./settings.html) — make paths
  resolve and set defaults.
